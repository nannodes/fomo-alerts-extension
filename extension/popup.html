FOMO Alerts

Take-profit and stop-loss alerts for fomo.family, sent to your Telegram.

  1. Log in on fomo.familyOpen fomo.family in this browser and log in to your account.
  2. Open FOMO AlertsClick your profile icon at the top right, then FOMO Alerts. Link your Telegram there.
  3. Set your alertsOn any coin page, under the Buy/Sell box. Your alerts are also in the FOMO Alerts tab of the side panel.
